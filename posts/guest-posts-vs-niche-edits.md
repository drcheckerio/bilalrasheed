Guest posts and niche edits are the two most common ways to build links through outreach. They are often sold as rivals. In practice a healthy link profile has both.

## What each one is

A **guest post** is a new article written for another site, with a link back to yours. A **niche edit** (also called a link insertion) is a link added to an article that already exists on another site.

## How they compare

- **Speed.** Niche edits are faster, because the page is already published and indexed.
- **Control.** Guest posts give you control over the whole article, so the link sits in content built around your topic.
- **Page strength.** An existing article may already have links and traffic of its own. A new guest post starts from zero.
- **Relevance.** A guest post can be written to match your page exactly. A niche edit depends on finding an article that already fits.

## Which to use first

Start with guest posts if your site is new and you need topical relevance around your main pages. Add niche edits when you want to strengthen pages that are already close to ranking.

Whichever you choose, the same checks apply: a real site, real traffic, a relevant topic and anchor text that reads naturally.
