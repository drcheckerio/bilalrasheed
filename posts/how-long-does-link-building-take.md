The short answer is **two to three months for most sites**, and longer in competitive niches. Here is what is happening while you wait.

## Why links are not instant

A new link has to be crawled, indexed and weighed against everything else Google knows about both pages. That takes days for some links and weeks for others. Rankings then shift gradually as more links are counted.

## What changes the timeline

- **Your starting point.** A site with some authority reacts faster than a brand new domain.
- **The competition.** Outranking pages with hundreds of referring domains takes more links and more time.
- **The page you link to.** Links cannot rescue thin content. Strong on-page SEO makes every link work harder.
- **Consistency.** A steady flow of links each month looks natural and compounds. A single burst fades.

## What to watch while you wait

Rankings are the last thing to move. Before that you should see new referring domains in your backlink tool, more impressions in Search Console and pages climbing from page three to page two.

If none of those move after three months, review the quality of the links and the pages they point to before buying more.
