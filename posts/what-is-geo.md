People now ask AI assistants the questions they used to type into a search box. The assistant reads several sources, writes one answer and names a few of those sources. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the work of becoming one of the sources it names.

## How GEO differs from SEO

SEO earns a position in a list of links. GEO earns a mention inside a written answer. The two share a foundation, since AI systems lean heavily on pages that already rank and on brands that are mentioned across the web.

| | SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank in search results | Be cited in AI answers |
| Unit of success | A click | A mention or citation |
| Main signals | Relevance, links, experience | Clarity, authority, mentions across the web |

## Where to start

1. **Answer the question first.** Open each section with a direct, quotable answer, then add the detail.
2. **Use clear structure.** Descriptive headings, short paragraphs, lists and tables are easier for a model to lift from.
3. **Add facts a model can cite.** Original numbers, definitions and step-by-step processes get quoted more than opinion.
4. **Earn mentions on other sites.** Digital PR and quality links matter here too, because assistants trust brands they see in many reliable places.
5. **Mark up your pages.** Schema helps machines understand who you are and what the page covers.

## The short version

Good GEO is good SEO written more clearly and backed by a stronger brand. If you already invest in useful content and real links, you are most of the way there.
